No Secretions

We skip dairy, honey, and eggs because we know too much about how they’re made. It’s not that we don’t like them. We’re physically repulsed.

A cow makes milk in her udder to feed her calf. See how it’s made

Bees pass nectar mouth to mouth, then dry it in the comb to make honey for the hive. See how it’s made

Eggs are just gross. Inside a hen, the yolk forms first. The white and shell form around it before she lays the egg. See how they’re made

We eat meat and plants. A No Secretions sticker beside a dish means its maker has checked the whole recipe against our ingredient guide.

Common questions

So, is this an actual diet?+

We call this way of eating anti-secretionism. Someone who follows it can say, “I’m an anti-secretionist. I eat meat and skip dairy, honey and eggs.”

Does dairy include butter and cheese?+

Yes. Milk, butter, cheese, cream, dairy yogurt, ghee, whey, and other dairy ingredients are off the menu.

What about plant milk and maple syrup?+

They’re welcome. Choose versions without eggs, dairy, or honey. Plant-based yogurt and butter alternatives follow the same rule.

What do I say at a restaurant?+

“I eat meat and plants. I skip eggs, dairy, and honey. Could you cook mine in oil and check the breading, sauce, and glaze?” You can copy that explanation below.

Can I support this if I eat dairy or honey?+

Absolutely. You can love both and still want your friends to find a meal they can order. Add your name to the menu petition.
