Run a restaurant? Start with one dish.

Try the label at your restaurant

You can use the phrase “Anti-secretionist friendly” on your own menus for free, starting today. Your kitchen stands behind the ingredient check.

  1. Choose one dish. Trace the recipe from prep to plate. Check the supplier’s ingredients for bread, breading, marinades, glazes and sauces. Include cooking fats, garnishes and anything brushed on before serving.

  2. Check each ingredient. Meat and plants fit. Exclude eggs and egg-derived ingredients, dairy and milk derivatives, honey, royal jelly, beeswax and shellac. Keep the dish off the labeled list while any ingredient is unconfirmed. Read the full ingredient standard.

  3. Label the exact order. Put the wording beside the dish on your printed and online menus. If it only qualifies with a swap, name the swap and make it selectable wherever customers order. Record cross-contact information separately. Diners with an allergy should consult your allergen guidance.

  4. Keep the label current. Note who checked the dish and when. Take the label down if a supplier, recipe or preparation changes, and check it again before putting it back.
