FIELD NOTES

The no-cheese pizza order

The easiest pizza order starts before you pick toppings. You can take mozzarella off the top of a pie. You can’t take honey out of dough that was mixed this morning.

Start with the dough

Ask whether the dough contains egg, milk, butter or honey. A server might know the dough recipe by heart. If the shop buys dough, ask whether the ingredient label is handy. If nobody can check during a Friday rush, try the question by phone before your next visit.

Follow the pizza to the oven

Choose a red sauce and ask whether the kitchen mixes in butter or grated cheese. Then pick toppings you actually like. Mushroom, olive and roasted pepper give you plenty to bite into. If you want sausage, ask what’s in the sausage itself.

Remember the last thirty seconds

A pizza can leave the oven exactly as ordered and still get a brush of garlic butter, a sprinkle of Parmesan or a spiral of hot honey on its way to the box. Ask for plain olive oil if you want a finish.

Could I get red sauce, mushrooms and olives, with no cheese? I skip eggs, dairy, and honey. Could you check the dough and sauce, and leave off any butter or honey finish?
