Meet Gemmy.

Gemmy has her binoculars up. What’s she looking for? A place where her family can sit down together and she can order a dinner she likes.

She likes meat and vegetables cooked without butter, bread made without milk, and pie without glaze. Somebody else at the table can have butter on theirs.

Ask the kitchen together

For the main course, try “Could you cook the steak and vegetables in olive oil?” At the bakery, “What goes into the dough?” helps you check for milk, eggs and honey. If Gemmy spots a pie she likes, she asks about both the crust and what was brushed on top.
