What a reviewed tag would mean

A reviewer checks the current formula and ingredient statement for a specific product and pack size against a published list. Eggs and egg-derived ingredients, dairy and milk derivatives, honey, royal jelly, beeswax and shellac are excluded. Meat and plant ingredients are permitted. Unresolved ingredients keep the product pending. Cross-contact statements are recorded separately.

A production tag would identify the SKU, review date and a link or QR code to its record. A changed formula triggers a fresh review and tag withdrawal until it passes. This sample links to the proposed standard. No products have been certified.

Run a small pilot

Start with one grocer, ten pantry staples and a four-week display. Compare unit sales with the prior four weeks and a similar shelf without tags. Track scans and staff questions. Brands can fund review time. The same criteria apply to everyone.
