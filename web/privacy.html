What we keep.

The label checker runs on your device. You can sign the petition without giving a name or email. When you sign, your browser saves a random code and sends it to us so we can recognize your signature on your next visit. Clearing site data or switching browsers can reset that recognition. If you’re signed in, we also use your site-specific account identifier to recognize earlier signatures. Only the total signature count is public.

For interest votes, we store your site-specific account identifier and your choice.

The host handles sign-in. Merchant checkout is closed. If advertising launches, Google may use cookies or similar technologies for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Consent options must be configured before ads launch. How Google uses data on partner sites.
