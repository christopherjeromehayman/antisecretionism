FIELD NOTES

Breakfast in Los Gatos

A good starting order is crisp bacon, breakfast potatoes and fruit. These two local menus give you a place to start. The menus establish which dishes are offered. Confirm the ingredients and cooking fat with the kitchen when you order.

Southern Kitchen

27 East Main Street. Ask about ordering bacon and hash browns as sides. Check whether the potatoes contain egg or dairy, whether the bacon has a honey glaze, and whether the kitchen can use oil on the griddle.

Southern Kitchen breakfast menu

Los Gatos Cafe Downtown

340 North Santa Cruz Avenue. Breakfast is served all day. Ask which potato side the kitchen can prepare with oil. If you want toast, check the dough and glaze for eggs, dairy, and honey. Fruit makes an easy alternative.

Los Gatos Cafe · Downtown ordering menu

Say it at the table

Could I get extra-crispy bacon, potatoes cooked in oil, and a side of fruit? I skip eggs, dairy, and honey. Could you check the bacon glaze and what goes into the potatoes?
