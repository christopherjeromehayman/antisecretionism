Label one dish.

Try it in your kitchen

  1. Check the whole plate. Check the marinade, cooking fat, sauce, sides, and garnish, including ingredients supplied by someone else. The potatoes count, too.
  2. Label the option you checked. If the dish needs a swap, make it selectable when guests order. Add an option on DoorDash.
  3. Send a test order. Choose the swap on your ordering page. Read the ticket with the cook to make sure the instruction comes through.

Recheck the dish when a recipe, supplier, or preparation changes. Keep your usual allergen and cross-contact guidance available.

No Secretions

This sticker means the food, as offered, meets our ingredient guidelines. We skip dairy, honey, other ingredients made from animal secretions, and eggs.

For a pastry, check the butter in the dough, the filling, the egg wash, and the glaze. If the whole recipe fits, put No Secretions beside the pastry’s name, wherever you already show dietary labels.

Use the sticker on your website

Upload the PNG to your ordering site and give it the image description “No Secretions.” A text label works too. Link either to this guide so diners can see what you checked.

Friend of Gemmy

Display Gemmy if your team is happy to take ingredient questions, check with the kitchen, and tell guests which dishes or swaps you can offer. Here’s a line you can use on your ordering page.

“We’re a Friend of Gemmy. If you skip dairy, honey, and eggs, tell us what looks good. We’ll check how it’s made and what we can change for you.”
