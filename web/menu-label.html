Label a dish or product

The welcome badge describes your team. Use Anti-secretionist friendly beside an individual dish or packaged product once you’ve checked its recipe. Your kitchen or brand stands behind that check.

  • Check the whole recipe. Meat and plants can fit. Check bread, sauces, cooking fats, garnishes, and glazes for eggs, dairy, honey, royal jelly, beeswax, and shellac, including ingredients derived from them. Confirm anything unclear with the supplier.
  • Describe the exact order. If a dish needs a substitution, name it beside the dish and offer it wherever customers order. For example, “Grilled steak — anti-secretionist friendly when ordered with olive oil in place of butter.”
  • Keep it current. For packaged food, check the specific product and flavor. Review the label again when ingredients, suppliers, or preparation change. Keep allergen and cross-contact guidance available separately.
