No Secretions

This sticker means the food, as offered, meets our ingredient guidelines. We skip dairy, honey, other ingredients made from animal secretions, and eggs.

For a pastry, check the butter in the dough, the filling, the egg wash, and the glaze. If the whole recipe fits, put No Secretions beside the pastry’s name, wherever you already show dietary labels.

Friend of Gemmy

Display Gemmy if your team is happy to take ingredient questions, check with the kitchen, and tell guests which dishes or swaps you can offer. Here’s a line you can use on your ordering page.

“We’re a Friend of Gemmy. If you skip dairy, honey, and eggs, tell us what looks good. We’ll check how it’s made and what we can change for you.”

Try it with one dish

Pick something your kitchen already makes well. A loaf of bread might fit as baked. A steak might fit when the cook uses olive oil. Here’s how to get that exact dish from the menu to the table.

  • Check the whole plate. Include the marinade, cooking fat, sauce, sides, and garnish. Check supplier ingredients against the ingredient guide. Label the dish once you can account for each ingredient.
  • Make the swap selectable. If your steak fits only with olive oil, add a “Cooking fat” choice at checkout. Give diners two options, “Butter” and “Olive oil, no butter.” Put the item label on the checked option. How to add a menu option on DoorDash.
  • Read the kitchen ticket. Place a test order through your ordering page and choose the swap. Check that the cook sees “Olive oil, no butter” on the ticket. Show the front-of-house team where to find that same choice.
  • Keep the details current. Recheck the dish when a recipe, supplier, or preparation changes. For packaged foods, check each product and flavor. Keep your usual allergen and cross-contact guidance available.
